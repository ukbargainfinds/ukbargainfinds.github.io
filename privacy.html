Privacy

This page explains what happens to your information when you visit UK Bargain Finds.

What this site collects

Nothing. There are no forms, accounts, sign-ups, comments, analytics or tracking scripts, and this site sets no cookies of its own.

Hosting

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical details such as your IP address when you visit, for security and to keep the service running. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Product photos

Product photos load directly from the shops' own websites, so those shops can see that your browser requested the image.

Affiliate links

When you click a deal you leave this site. The shop, and for affiliate links the affiliate network (for example Awin, Tradedoubler or Webgains), may set cookies so the shop can credit a sale. Their own privacy and cookie policies apply from that point. I only ever see commission totals, never who bought what.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law (UK GDPR) you can ask what personal data is held about you. As this site holds none, there is normally nothing to share or delete. If you have a question, get in touch through the Contact page. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.
